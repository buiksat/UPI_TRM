This ledger distinguishes a mathematical implication from evidence that its
assumptions hold in an implementation. An artifact being present does not
establish its execution provenance. Code-facing rows remain open until the
post-repair test and evidence audit is complete.

## Status vocabulary

- `proved`: a proof is present and has survived the current mathematical audit, subject to any repair stated in the row.
- `verified from artifact`: the displayed result can be recomputed from a retained artifact. This does not imply a valid experimental comparison.
- `finite-batch diagnostic only`: the quantity is an empirical statistic on retained states, not a uniform theorem quantity.
- `historical record only`: retained output without enough provenance for a corrected rerun or causal comparison.
- `not verifiable from supplied evidence`: the required artifact or provenance is absent.
- `contradicted by implementation`: the inspected implementation violates the stated condition.
- `missing experiment`: no completed experiment addresses the claim.

No current anonymous supplement archive exists. Statements below refer to files in the working tree, not to a submitted bundle.

`CODE_REPO/` denotes the implementation repository. This logical prefix avoids embedding workstation identity in a release-facing report.
